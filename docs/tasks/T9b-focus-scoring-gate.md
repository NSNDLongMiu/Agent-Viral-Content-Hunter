## Problems found on the real run (2026-11-22)
1. **Substring bug.** `RuleScorer._score_relevance` tests `kw in text_corpus` (plain substring). The topic keyword `"ai"` therefore matches inside "Tr**ai**tors", "s**ai**d", "m**ai**l"… The TV show "The Traitors: New Blood" got `+25 chủ đề ưu tiên: ai` and became the only P2 of the run (it was even sent to the user's Telegram). Vietnamese also uses `ai` as a pronoun ("ai cũng biết").
2. **No focus on the owner's niche.** The product owner wants short films, micro-dramas, entertainment, oriented to content that can be made with generative AI. Nothing tags or favours those topics, and the draft gate (virality ≥ 60 AND relevance ≥ 45) blocks them: news items from RSS feeds have no engagement metrics, so their virality is never ≥ 60.
